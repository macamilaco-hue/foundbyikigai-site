2. Information We Collect

Information you provide

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Your name.
  • Your email address.

  • Information about your interests, values, strengths, experiences, goals, priorities, relationships, and areas of personal satisfaction.

Your answers may include personal or sensitive information. Please do not submit information that is unnecessary for receiving the service, including government identification numbers, financial credentials, medical records, or formal medical diagnoses.

Payment information

Technical information

When you access the website, our hosting, security, and infrastructure providers may automatically process limited technical information necessary to deliver and protect the service, including:

3. How We Use Personal Information

We may process personal information for the following purposes:

  • To generate personalized questions based on your responses.
  • To analyze your responses using the Found by Ikigai framework.
  • To create personalized previews, results, and reports.

  • To communicate with you about your purchase, report, account, or support request.
  • To respond to questions, privacy requests, complaints, or inquiries.
  • To maintain, troubleshoot, secure, and improve the website and service.
  • To detect and prevent fraud, misuse, unauthorized access, and security incidents.
  • To maintain accounting, tax, transaction, and business records.
  • To comply with legal, regulatory, and contractual obligations.
  • To send marketing communications when you have authorized us to do so.

We will not use personal information for a materially different purpose without providing appropriate notice or obtaining authorization when required.

Found by Ikigai uses artificial intelligence and automated systems to:

  • Analyze written responses.
  • Identify themes and patterns.
  • Generate personalized follow-up questions.
  • Create personalized reports and reflective content.

Your responses may be processed using Anthropic’s Claude API or another AI service provider disclosed in this Privacy Policy.

These providers may process information on Found by Ikigai’s behalf to deliver the requested functionality. Their processing is also subject to their applicable contractual terms, privacy practices, security measures, and legal obligations.

Found by Ikigai may separately store your test responses and report in its own systems or through its hosting and database providers.

AI-generated content may contain errors, incomplete interpretations, or conclusions that do not apply to your circumstances.

The Found test and report:

  • Are intended for informational and personal-reflection purposes.
  • Do not constitute a medical or psychological diagnosis.
  • Do not replace therapy, counseling, medical treatment, or professional advice.
  • Are not used to make decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects concerning you.

You may withdraw your authorization when permitted by law. Withdrawal will not affect processing that took place before the request was received and may not apply where Found by Ikigai has a legal or contractual obligation to retain or process certain information.
